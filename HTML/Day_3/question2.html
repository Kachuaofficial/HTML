  • Cooking Steps
    1. In a cooking pot, bring lots of water with salt to boil (4-5 cups) firstly, for cooking pasta. If you taste the water, it should be salty like sea water. That's the common reference.
    2. After that, add pasta and boil until it's cooked al dente.
    3. Then drain in a colander and keep aside. Reserve ¼ cup water for later use.
    4. After that, in the same hot water, I added the tomato, keep it immersed for 20 seconds.
    5. Then take it out, peel the skin.
    6. After that, puree it in a mixie/ blender.

Finally, garnish with coriander leaves. Simple and easy Masala pasta is ready to serve!

Enjoy it hot for best taste. But you can definitely pack for lunch box too, it won't disappoint you.
