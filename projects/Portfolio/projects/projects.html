My Projects

Sahayak Ai

Sahayak AI is an AI-powered assistance platform designed to help village residents easily discover and understand government welfare schemes available to them. The project addresses the challenge of limited awareness and accessibility of government information in rural areas by providing simple, personalized, and easy-to-understand guidance.

Smart Academic Assitant

Smart Academic Assistant is a web application that helps students to manage their academic information. It is a tool that can be used to track student progress, assignments, and grades.

Civic Seva

Civic Seva is a application that helps citizens to report issues in their community. It is a tool that can be used to report issues such as potholes, broken streetlights, and other public infrastructure problems.

Turtle

Turtle is a gamified quiz application that help learners to revise topics and questions in ease and with the help of AI
